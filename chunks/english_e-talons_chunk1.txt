An e-ticket is a unified electronic ticket for Riga city public transport, which ensures payment for trips. A purchased ticket in an e-ticket is valid for 12 months from the moment of its purchase, unless the ticket tariff has been changed or the ticket type has been canceled. In such cases, "Rīgas satiksme" provides a transition period for the validity of the ticket. An e-ticket must not be bent, pressed, broken, creased, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable; How to use an e-ticket: * When boarding public transport - a bus, trolleybus or tram, the e-ticket must be brought closer to the electronic card reader. This way the trip is registered, and the passenger pays for his trip. Trip registration must also be carried out by those passengers who use 100% fare concessions. All types of e-tickets are described below: Personalized e-ticket: * made of plastic; * valid for 10 years; * tied to only one user; * contains customer data – photo card, name, surname; * can only be used by the card owner; * mandatory for those who use discounts on public transport, except for those who use the state-set 100% fare relief (group 1 and 2 disabled people and persons with disabilities up to 18 years of age; see here), but any passenger can apply by going to the customer center and filling out the application form; * in case of loss, it is possible to block and restore without losing paid services; * tickets can be purchased at more than 400 points of sale (see points of sale here); * tickets must be used within 12 months from the date of purchase, except if the ticket type tariff changes or if the ticket type is canceled and "Rīgas satiksme" has determined a transition period for the validity of the ticket type; * the e-ticket must not be bent, pressed, broken, creased, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable; * the first issuance is free of charge, re-issuance of an e-ticket in case of loss costs EUR 2.85; * a questionnaire must be filled out to receive an e-ticket; Non-personalized e-ticket: * made of plastic; * valid for 10 years; * the validity period of a non-personalized e-ticket can be found at "Rīgas satiksme" customer centers and ticket sales points; * does not contain customer data – can be used by several passengers; * can be purchased at "Rīgas satiksme" customer centers, destinations, as well as at a ticket sales point if the customer purchases at least 10 time tickets at the same time; * at the time of receiving the e-ticket, the customer pays a guarantee fee of 2.85EUR (including VAT), which can be refunded if the e-ticket is returned in good condition within 6 months after the expiration date.
