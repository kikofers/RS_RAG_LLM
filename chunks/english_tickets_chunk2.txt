Price EUR: 12.00. The monthly ticket is valid for one month from the first day of registration. The number of trips is not limited. A full-price monthly ticket is available on the Riga Citizen's Card, personalized, non-personalized and yellow e-ticket, as well as in the code ticket app. A monthly ticket with an appropriate Riga City Council fare discount is available on the Riga Citizen's Card and on a personalized e-ticket. The ticket loaded on the e-ticket must be used within 12 months of purchase, while the code ticket must be used within 3 months of purchase, except in cases where the tariff changes and a transition period is established. Information for persons with disability groups I and II, persons with disabilities under the age of 18 and persons accompanying a person with disability group I or a person with disabilities under the age of 18*. Persons with disability groups I and II and persons with disabilities under the age of 18 must register each trip using a personalized e-ticket (it can be made free of charge at "Rīgas satiksme" customer service centers, upon presentation of an identity document and a disability certificate. If a person with disability groups I and II or a person with disabilities under the age of 18 does not have a personalized e-ticket, it is necessary to obtain a driving certificate from the driver. Persons with disability group III can use the fare discount by registering a personalized e-ticket to which the discount is attached.
