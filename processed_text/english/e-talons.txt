An e-ticket is a unified electronic ticket for Riga city public transport, which ensures payment for trips. A purchased ticket in an e-ticket is valid for 12 months from the moment of its purchase, unless the ticket tariff has been changed or the ticket type has been canceled. In such cases, "Rīgas satiksme" provides a transition period for the validity of the ticket.
An e-ticket must not be bent, pressed, broken, creased, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable;
How to use an e-ticket:
* When boarding public transport - a bus, trolleybus or tram, the e-ticket must be brought closer to the electronic card reader. This way the trip is registered, and the passenger pays for his trip. Trip registration must also be carried out by those passengers who use 100% fare concessions.

All types of e-tickets are described below:

Personalized e-ticket:
* made of plastic;
* valid for 10 years;
* tied to only one user;
* contains customer data – photo card, name, surname;
* can only be used by the card owner;
* mandatory for those who use discounts on public transport, except for those who use the state-set 100% fare relief (group 1 and 2 disabled people and persons with disabilities up to 18 years of age; see here), but any passenger can apply by going to the customer center and filling out the application form;
* in case of loss, it is possible to block and restore without losing paid services;
* tickets can be purchased at more than 400 points of sale (see points of sale here);
* tickets must be used within 12 months from the date of purchase, except if the ticket type tariff changes or if the ticket type is canceled and "Rīgas satiksme" has determined a transition period for the validity of the ticket type;
* the e-ticket must not be bent, pressed, broken, creased, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable;
* the first issuance is free of charge, re-issuance of an e-ticket in case of loss costs EUR 2.85;
* a questionnaire must be filled out to receive an e-ticket;

Non-personalized e-ticket:
* made of plastic;
* valid for 10 years;
* the validity period of a non-personalized e-ticket can be found at "Rīgas satiksme" customer centers and ticket sales points;
* does not contain customer data – can be used by several passengers;
* can be purchased at "Rīgas satiksme" customer centers, destinations, as well as at a ticket sales point if the customer purchases at least 10 time tickets at the same time;
* at the time of receiving the e-ticket, the customer pays a guarantee fee of 2.85EUR (including VAT), which can be refunded if the e-ticket is returned in good condition within 6 months after the expiration date. In turn, it is issued free of charge if tickets are purchased for at least 15EUR;
* suitable for customers who regularly use public transport, including monthly subscription tickets without discounts, and do not want to use a personalized e-ticket;
* in case of loss, it is impossible to block and restore;

Riga Resident Card:
* The card can be received by persons declared in Riga or persons who have property in Riga;
* the first issuance is free of charge, the production of a repeated Riga Resident Card in case of loss costs EUR 2.85;
* valid for 10 years;
* tied to one user;
* contains customer data – photo card, name, surname, personal identification number;
* in case of loss, it is possible to block and restore without losing paid services;
* tickets can be purchased at more than 400 points of sale (see points of sale here);
* monthly tickets and season tickets can be loaded;
* tickets must be used within 12 months from the date of purchase, except if the ticket type tariff changes or if the ticket type is cancelled and "Rīgas satiksme" has set a transition period for the validity of the ticket type;
* The Riga Resident Card must not be bent, pressed, broken, creased, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable;
* if the recipient is declared in Riga, then the Riga Resident Card can be obtained at the customer center (you can apply for a visit here);
* if the recipient has property in Riga, then the Riga Resident Card can be obtained at the customer center (you can apply for a visit here);
* a questionnaire must be filled out to receive an e-ticket;

Student card:
* intended as a student ID card for Riga City Municipality general education institutions;
* grants users the right to receive benefits, services and advantages of Riga City Municipality, municipal capital companies (Riga institutions) and municipal cooperation partners;
* is issued to persons who are studying an educational program at a Riga City Municipality general education institution - regardless of their declared place of residence;
* is issued to persons who are studying an educational program at a general education institution registered outside Riga, but whose place of residence is declared in the administrative territory of Riga City;
* is tied to one user;
* contains customer data - photo card, name, surname, personal identification number;
* can only be used by the card owner;
* the validity period is indicated on the card or the validity period of the card expires when the student graduates from school;
* made of plastic;
* in case of loss, it is possible to block and restore it;
* the student e-card must not be bent, pressed, broken, wrinkled, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable;
* a questionnaire must be filled out to receive an e-ticket;
* the first issuance is free of charge, repeated production of a student e-card in case of loss costs EUR 2.85.

Companion card:
* made of plastic;
* valid for 10 years;
* issued to a person with a group 1 disability and a child with a disability;
* contains personal data - the personal identification number and photograph of the person with a disability;
* the companion card may be used when traveling in public transport together with the person with a disability to whom the companion card was issued;
* the hole made in the middle of the companion card helps visually impaired passengers distinguish the card from other types of e-tickets;
* in case of loss, it is possible to block and restore it;
* the card can be issued at “Rīgas satiksme” customer centers (see here);
* the card must not be bent, pressed, broken, creased, soaked or subjected to other similar actions that make it unusable;
* the first issuance is free of charge, re-issuance of an e-ticket in case of loss costs EUR 2.85;

Legal entity customer card
* made of plastic;
* valid for 10 years;
* non-personalized (does not include personal data);
* issued to legal entities;
* Tickets must be used within 12 months from the date of purchase, except if the fare of the ticket type changes or if the ticket type is cancelled and "Rīgas satiksme" has determined a transition period for the validity of the ticket type;
* For customers with whom a ticket purchase agreement has been concluded, the first issuance of cards for use is free of charge, the first fee for customers without an agreement is 2.85;
* Re-issuance of an e-ticket in case of loss or damage costs EUR 2.85.

Yellow e-ticket:
* made of cardboard, with an electronic chip;
* it is possible to purchase monthly, seasonal and daily tickets;
* tickets can be purchased at more than 400 points of sale;
* convenient for passengers who use Riga's public transport irregularly, including guests of Riga;
* tickets must be used within 12 months from the moment of purchase, except if the ticket type tariff changes or the ticket type is canceled and "Rīgas satiksme" has determined a transition period for the validity of the ticket type;
* the e-ticket is intended for registration of a single person's trip.
