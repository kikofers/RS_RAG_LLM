About stops on request:
"Rīgas satiksme" trolleybus routes 1, 3, 4, 5, 16, 19, 27, 31, 34 and 35, as well as 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 36, 38, 39, 43, 44, 46, 47, 48, 49, 50, On bus routes 51, 52, 54, 55, 56, 57, 58, 60 and 63 outside the central part of Riga, a "stop on request" has been introduced. This means that the bus driver stops at such a stop only if the passenger expresses his desire to get off in time by pressing the door opening button or, while at the stop, is in a place visible to the driver and stands in the stop sign zone.
